Our Team

Gopi Prasannaa

Founder / CEO

Gopi is a rare jack-of-all-trades and master of them all. As creator, innovator, developer, artist, designer, and sculptor, he can whip up a print ad, develop a game, create an animated character, design a movie poster, redo your living room, or paint you a picture. CNN dubbed him a 21st Century Visionary when his brainchild, the iBangle, won Best Product of the Year 2008.

Today, he is a prolific publicity designer, having designed posters across Tamil, Telugu, Hindi, and Malayalam cinema. Gopi's foray into the production design space kicked off with Amazon Prime's Modern Love Chennai, an anthology of six films with six unique geographies, colours, and architectural styles.

At the heart of it all, he is a reticent artist who firmly believes that it is art that speaks where words fail.

S V Udayakumar

Chief Design Officer

Udaya Kumar is a design leader with over a decade of experience in brand, film, and spatial design. He holds a Master’s degree in Sculpture from the Government College of Fine Arts, Chennai, and has led creative direction for more than 100+ film publicity projects and numerous brand campaigns. As Creative Director at Gopi Prasannaa Studio, he has shaped the visual language of major films and collaborated with leading brands, including DELL, Flying Machine, McDowell's, and the Murugappa Group.

His design journey spans art direction, animation, and spatial concepts, with early experience assisting on interior design projects across India. Udaya's expertise in stop-motion animation and visual storytelling has also contributed to ad campaigns, such as the Stayzilla commercial directed by Thiagarajan Kumararaja. With a foundation in fine arts and hands-on experience across mediums, he continues to lead teams and deliver impactful visual solutions across industries.

Vasan S

VP – Technology

Vasan S is an accomplished entrepreneur with a strong foundation in computer science, holding a degree from IIT Madras. With early career stints at New York Stock Exchange and Enguage in Atlanta, he returned to India to pursue his entrepreneurial ambitions in the tech and product innovation space.

He is the founder of 1000 Lookz, a pioneering virtual makeover platform that enables users to preview cosmetics, hair colour, and eyewear using real-time imaging, a first-of-its-kind in India. The platform has collaborated with over 40 leading brands and e-commerce companies and was part of the Microsoft Accelerator program.

Vasan has filed 18 patents and has been widely recognised for his innovation. His accolades include the CII Emerging Entrepreneur of the Year (2016), NASSCOM Top 10 Product Companies (2015), and multiple honours from Microsoft, IAMAI, and Indira International.

Vasan continues to focus on deep tech products that blend AI, retail, and user experience in impactful ways.

Aravind Naga

COO / Head of Video Production

A storyteller at heart, Aravind has always believed that every idea deserves to be seen, not just heard. This belief has led to a career that blends creativity with sharp technical know-how.

Over the past decade, he has worked across visual effects, post-production, and filmmaking, diving into feature films, web series, brand campaigns and immersive digital experiences. This mix has given him a rare ability to bring cinematic thinking into the fast-paced world of advertising and media.

Today, as COO and Head of Video Production at Magic In White, Aravind brings people, ideas, and tech together, ensuring that every project moves smoothly from concept to screen. He continues to explore new possibilities as a filmmaker, VFX supervisor, and Unreal Engine Authorized Instructor.

Always curious, always experimenting, and always rooting for the next generation of storytellers.

Vasumathi

Chief Marketing Officer

Vasumathi brings together strategy, storytelling, and an instinct for what truly connects with audiences. Eight years in the world of film marketing, brand strategy, and entertainment communications have seen her navigating everything from theatrical releases to celebrity narratives and digital-led brand building.

As the former CEO of an entertainment marketing agency, she blends data-driven insight with creative thinking and collaboration, ensuring ideas translate into real impact. Over the years, she has partnered with brands like Volvo, Athulya, Kauvery Hospitals, Credo Health App, Credai CasaGrand, and Bashyam.

At the heart of it all, Vasumathi believes marketing is not just about being seen, but about being remembered.

Vinoth C

Chief Photographer

Vinoth Chandrasekaran lives and breathes photography. After graduating from college, he honed his craft under the mentorship of Sharad Haksar, one of India’s most awarded advertising photographers. His decade-long stint at Evelight Pictures was a deep dive into the fundamentals of photography, mastering lighting, composition, point of view, and post-production with an artist’s eye and an advertiser’s precision.

As a core contributor to One Eyeland, Vinoth had the opportunity to study the works of world-renowned photographers. Vinoth’s signature style combines technical precision with creative originality. His work has earned international recognition, including awards and honourable mentions. Today, he brings that same blend of insight, craft, and creativity to every project he undertakes.

Uttam Kumar M

Head of Web & UX

Uttam has always been drawn to the challenge of making digital experiences simple, intuitive, and functional. Over the past decade, he has worked across platforms and products, bringing together design, technology, and business needs to create experiences that feel both functional and emotionally engaging.

Uttam is known for guiding creative teams with ease, simplifying complex design systems, and raising the bar for digital experience design. Under his leadership, the studio's UI/UX practice continues to grow, turning ideas into meaningful, well-crafted products that genuinely connect brands with people.
